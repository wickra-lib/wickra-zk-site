# About Wickra ZK

Zero-knowledge, on-chain-verifiable backtest performance — without revealing your data or your strategy.

## What it does

Wickra ZK runs a deterministic Wickra backtest inside a zero-knowledge circuit and produces a proof that a stated result was reached — without publishing the strategy specification or the underlying data.

## Why it exists

The two things people want from a track record are in tension: it should be checkable, and the edge behind it should stay private. A proof resolves the tension instead of trading one away.
